Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This policy adds to our Privacy Policy. It covers consumer health data as defined by Washington’s My Health My Data Act and similar state laws.

Health data we collect

  • Meals you log: food names, amounts, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, meal type, date and time.
  • Your daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat goals.
  • Meal descriptions, photos and voice transcripts you send for AI estimates. These go from your iPhone to OpenAI; we don’t store them.

Why we collect it and how we use it

To provide calmera: to save and show your meals, goals and progress, and to estimate nutrition when you ask. We don’t use it for advertising and don’t sell it.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, through the app.

Who we share it with

Service providers
Neon (database hosting) and Render (server hosting), which store and process it only to run calmera.
OpenAI
At your direction, through your ChatGPT account, to estimate nutrition.

Your rights

You can confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, see it and a list of everyone we’ve shared it with, delete it, and withdraw your consent. Export your data and delete your account from Settings, or contact us. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and if the appeal is denied you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.
