Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy adds to our Privacy Policy. It covers consumer health data as defined by Washington’s My Health My Data Act and similar state laws.

This policy covers the iPhone version that stores the journal on your device. If you used an earlier version with a Calmera account, contact calmeradev@gmail.com about that account or its records. Erasing this iPhone does not erase records held by an earlier service.

Health data calmera handles

  • Meals you log: food names, amounts, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, meal type, date and time. They stay on your iPhone.
  • Your daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat goals. They stay on your iPhone.
  • Meal descriptions, photos and voice transcripts you send for AI estimates. These go from your iPhone to the AI provider you choose, OpenAI or Anthropic; calmera doesn’t store them. Online search is on by default. Your AI provider may use details from your request, such as a restaurant or brand name, in a web search. You can turn it off in Settings → AI connection.

Why calmera uses it

To provide calmera: to show your meals, goals and progress, and to estimate nutrition when you ask. We don’t use it for advertising and don’t sell it.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, through the app.

Who receives it

When you request an estimate, the AI provider you connect receives the selected meal details under your account. Calmera’s publisher does not receive your journal or the estimate request.

OpenAI
At your direction, through your ChatGPT account or your own OpenAI API key, to estimate nutrition.
Anthropic
At your direction, through your own Anthropic API key, to estimate nutrition.

Storage and deletion

Your journal is excluded from iCloud Backup. You can export meals and current goals to a destination you choose. Erasing the journal leaves existing exports and provider records separate. Your provider’s retention and deletion terms apply to the requests it receives.

Privacy Policy

Your rights

Your health data is on your iPhone: you can see it in calmera, export it from Settings, erase it with Settings → Erase all data, and withdraw your consent by disconnecting your AI provider or erasing all data. Contact us with any request. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and if the appeal is denied you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.
