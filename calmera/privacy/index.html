Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

calmera is a meal-logging app made by Yufeng Wu (“calmera”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what calmera keeps, where it keeps it, who receives information, and the choices you have.

This policy covers the iPhone version that stores the journal on your device. If you used an earlier version with a Calmera account, contact calmeradev@gmail.com about that account or its records. Erasing this iPhone does not erase records held by an earlier service.

The short version

  • calmera keeps your meals, goals and settings on your iPhone. There’s no calmera account, and we don’t run servers that receive them.
  • To estimate nutrition, calmera sends what you type, photos you choose and the text of what you say to the AI provider you choose: OpenAI (through your ChatGPT account or your own OpenAI API key) or Anthropic (through your own Anthropic API key). Your voice recording stays on your iPhone.
  • Online search is on by default. Your AI provider may use details from your request, such as a restaurant or brand name, in a web search. You can turn it off in Settings → AI connection.
  • We don’t sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • You can export your meals and current goals or erase Calmera’s local data in Settings.

What calmera keeps on your iPhone

Meals
For each meal you save: the food name, amount, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, meal type, and the date and time.
Goals
Your daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat goals, and the date each one starts.
Start date
The day you started using calmera.
Preferences
Settings such as theme, voice language and food icons.
Consent
The version of this policy you agreed to, and when.
AI connection
Your ChatGPT sign-in or API key, kept in the iPhone’s secure Keychain.
AI usage
For each AI request in the last 7 days: when it was made, the provider and model, and how many tokens and web searches it used, so you can see what your AI use costs. Never what you logged.

Calmera excludes the journal from iCloud Backup. To keep a separate copy of your meals and current goals, use Settings → Export your data. You choose where the export goes. Your AI credentials are stored in the Keychain for this iPhone.

Information calmera doesn’t keep

  • Voice recordings. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone; the recording isn’t saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your goal survey answers, such as age, sex, height, weight and activity. Only the goals you choose to save are kept.
  • Photos. Photos you choose to analyze go to your AI provider as described below; calmera doesn’t store them.

AI estimates by OpenAI or Anthropic

calmera uses AI models to estimate the nutrition in your meals. Meals you type or say and meal photos are sent to the AI provider you choose: OpenAI (through your ChatGPT account or your own OpenAI API key) or Anthropic (through your own Anthropic API key). Requests go from your iPhone directly to that provider under your account. calmera has no servers of its own, so nothing passes through us.

What is sent:

  • The text you type about a meal.
  • Photos you choose to analyze.
  • The text of what you say when you log by voice.
  • Food names and amounts you ask calmera to estimate, and instructions written by calmera.

Online search is on by default. Your AI provider may use details from your request, such as a restaurant or brand name, in a web search. You can turn it off in Settings → AI connection.

The app sends the meal or food you ask about. Your full journal, goals and survey answers stay on the phone. Your provider authenticates each request under your account. When you sign in with ChatGPT, OpenAI also receives an identifier for this app installation. The app keeps the sign-in details it needs on this iPhone, including the email address OpenAI supplies to identify the connected account.

Calmera sets OpenAI’s response storage option to off. Providers may still retain inputs, outputs or request metadata. For OpenAI API keys, abuse-monitoring logs can contain content and are generally kept for up to 30 days. Anthropic normally deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days. Account agreements, legal requirements and safety investigations can change those periods. For ChatGPT sign-in, your OpenAI account terms and applicable data controls govern processing.

You can disconnect your AI provider in Settings at any time. AI estimates then stop, and calmera forgets your ChatGPT sign-in or API key on that iPhone.

How calmera uses information

  • To show your meals, goals and progress on your iPhone.
  • To estimate nutrition when you ask.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don’t use your information for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t use it to train AI models.

Who receives information

Calmera has no journal server. The publisher cannot access your saved journal.

The AI provider you choose receives what you send for estimates, at your direction, as described above.

If you write to us, we may disclose what you send if the law requires it, to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, us or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy keeps applying to it.

How long information stays

  • Your meals, goals and settings stay on your iPhone until you change or erase them, or delete calmera.
  • The AI usage record keeps only the last 7 days.
  • Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete them there.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic keep what they receive as their privacy policies and your account settings describe.

Your choices and rights

Export
Settings → Export your data.
Correct
Edit or delete any meal or goal in the app.
Erase
Settings → Erase all data removes the journal, preferences, consent and saved AI credentials from this iPhone. Erasing the app’s data does not delete requests already received by a provider. For those requests, use the provider’s privacy controls or contact the provider.
Withdraw consent
Disconnect your AI provider to stop AI estimates, or erase all data. calmera needs your agreement to this policy to work.

Because your information stays on your iPhone, you can review the journal yourself, export meals and current goals, and erase the local data. Depending on your state, you may have other rights too. Contact us with any request; we’ll answer within the time the law allows and never treat you differently for using these rights.

We don’t sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms.

Consumer health data

Meals and nutrition goals may be consumer health data under laws such as Washington’s My Health My Data Act. calmera keeps them on your iPhone, shares them only as this policy describes, with your consent, and never sells them.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Security

Your information stays on your iPhone, protected by iOS, and your ChatGPT sign-in or API key stays in the iPhone Keychain. Requests to OpenAI and Anthropic are encrypted with HTTPS. No method is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Children

calmera isn’t meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Where calmera is offered

calmera is free and offered only in the United States. We’re based in the United States, and OpenAI and Anthropic may process what you send them in the United States and other countries.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is material, the app will ask for your agreement before it applies to you.
